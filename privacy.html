1. Introduction & Purpose of VANTHESIS Calculus

VANTHESIS Calculus is an interactive educational web application designed for high school and university students and teachers. The platform provides symbolic calculus tools, function graphing, variation tables, trigonometric unit circle visualizers, 3D surfaces of revolution, and astronomical curve modeling. This Privacy Policy explains clearly what information we collect, how we use Google user data, how data is stored and protected, and how users can control or delete their data.

2. Google User Data Accessed & OAuth Scopes

VANTHESIS Calculus authenticates users strictly via official Google OAuth 2.0. We request only the minimum permissions necessary to operate the educational workspace:

Google OAuth Scope Data Accessed Specific Purpose in VANTHESIS Calculus
openid, email, profile Verified Google email address, display name, and profile avatar URL. Authenticating Student, Teacher, and Administrator sessions and displaying account identity inside the workspace.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file Read/write access strictly limited to files created directly by VANTHESIS Calculus (vanthesis_workspace_v1.json and, for the administrator only, vanthesis_system_master.json). Saving and synchronizing the user's own academic profile (role, full name, class, school, date of birth, gender) and saved calculus lessons across devices directly inside their personal Google Drive.

Strict Scope Boundary: VANTHESIS Calculus cannot and does not view, list, read, modify, or delete any other personal files, folders, documents, or photos in your Google Drive.

3. User-Provided Educational Profile Information

On first sign-in, students and teachers enter basic academic profile information (role, full name, class/subject, school name, date of birth, and gender) to support classroom organization and account verification. This information is stored inside the user's own vanthesis_workspace_v1.json file on their personal Google Drive and cached locally in their browser for session performance.

4. Data Sharing, Transfer & Limited Use Disclosure

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Disclosure:
VANTHESIS Calculus's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or disclose Google user data or personal profile information to any third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.
  • We do not use Google user data for targeted advertising, marketing profiling, or training generalized Artificial Intelligence (AI) or Machine Learning (ML) models.
  • VANTHESIS Calculus operates as a client-side serverless web application; your OAuth access token communicates directly between your browser and official Google API endpoints over HTTPS/TLS encryption.

5. Data Storage, Security, Retention & Deletion

  • Storage & Protection: User workspace files reside directly in each user's personal Google Drive protected by Google's infrastructure and HTTPS encryption. OAuth access tokens are kept only in temporary browser sessionStorage and are revoked immediately upon sign-out.
  • Retention: Because VANTHESIS Calculus does not maintain an external central database of user files, your data is retained only for as long as you keep the vanthesis_workspace_v1.json file in your Google Drive.
  • Deletion & Revocation: You have full control at any time to:
    1. Delete the file vanthesis_workspace_v1.json directly from your Google Drive.
    2. Revoke VANTHESIS Calculus's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
    3. Request removal of your email from any teacher/student approval list by emailing kientdvct@gmail.com (processed within 7 business days).
